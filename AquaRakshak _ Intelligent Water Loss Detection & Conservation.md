## Water Loss Often Goes Unnoticed.

### Hidden leaks

Undetected leaks may waste water before anyone notices.

### Little visibility

Limited consumption visibility makes unusual usage difficult to identify.

### Higher bills

Excessive water consumption can increase utility expenses.

### Resource pressure

Avoidable water loss places additional pressure on limited water resources.

## Meet AquaRakshak — From Water Data to Action.

- Records water consumption.
- Compares readings with expected usage.
- Flags unusual patterns.
- Helps you investigate potential leaks.
- Estimates water bills.
- Generates personalized conservation reports.

**Honest scope:** alerts come from a transparent rule (daily usage above 125% of your baseline). This is rule-based detection, not advanced AI, and it cannot confirm a physical leak.

### Tracking and keeping our own carbon footprint low

Saved water also means less pumping and treatment energy, so the calculator and report can estimate carbon from your usage when you enter an emission factor. The site itself is one light page with no photos, video, ads or tracking scripts, so each visit transfers little data. We have not measured the site's emissions and make no carbon claims for it.
